# spark-bootcamp

Five days from a raw idea to a first paying customer. A guided Claude Code sprint for founders,
built by [Spark AI Agency](https://sparkconsulting.tech) for Digital Jersey's cohort. Works for
software, hardware and services businesses.

## How it runs

Install 2 to 4 weeks before your bootcamp Monday, then run three commands in order. After that,
`coach` always tells you the one command to run next: first the pre-work chain that books your
Day 1 interviews (nobody is free tomorrow, so invitations go out weeks ahead), then the five days.

```
/spark-bootcamp:doctor    # once: checks your machine, lays out your project, prints the pre-work
/spark-bootcamp:start     # captures your idea, your path, your bootcamp Monday and your numeric target
/spark-bootcamp:coach     # any time you are lost: where you are, what is done, what to run next
```

Every skill talks before it writes, sized to the decision: the shaping work (your idea, your
interviews, your position, your price) is a proper conversation; technical documents get drafted
fast and then explained back in plain English so you can poke holes; research and assembly skills
ask two or three sharp questions and get on with it. "Skip" and "you decide" are always allowed,
and always logged, so no artefact hides a call you never saw.

Everything you produce is saved to your own project (`00-prework/` … `05-sale/`) and logged to
`CHANGELOG.md` and `DECISIONS.md`, so you end the week with an audit trail, not just a product.

## The 63 skills

### The spine (always on)
| skill | what it does |
|---|---|
| `doctor` | One-time setup and pre-work check: machine, accounts, trading entity and bank, domain, budget |
| `start` | Registers your name, path, one-sentence idea and headline numeric target |
| `coach` | Tells you where you are and the one next command |
| `checkpoint` | Opens each day with a target, closes it with the actual number |
| `journey-state` | The state-machine helper every skill reads and writes (hidden) |
| `logbook` | The one logging format for CHANGELOG and DECISIONS (hidden) |
| `audit-pack` | Compiles the week's logs into a regulator or investor pack |
| `brand-register` | Canonical brand store every document reads from |
| `house-style` | Shared deck and page templates plus the voice checklist (hidden) |
| `business-profile` | The one software/hardware/services variance matrix (hidden) |
| `guardrails` | No fabricated numbers, human sign-off before send or spend (hidden) |
| `regulator-check` | Reviews any artefact for unsupported claims before it ships |
| `data-protection` | Draft privacy notice, consent wording and data-processing clause |

### The interview engine (Day 1 and Day 4)
| skill | what it does |
|---|---|
| `interview-method` | The Mom Test, jobs-to-be-done and buying-signal knowledge base (hidden) |
| `run-interview` | Runs and captures one interview, discovery or product-test mode |

### Pre-work: book the interviews (2 to 4 weeks before the Monday)
| skill | what it does |
|---|---|

| `p0-schedule` | Tracks replies, chases silence, closes pre-work at 8+ booked |

## The three paths

One plugin, three paths, chosen in `start`. Build and ship skills branch on it, unified by "a
live page a real prospect can act on":

- **software**: a deployed working slice on a public URL (default stack Next.js + Supabase + Vercel).
- **hardware**: a prototype (CAD render or mock) plus a pre-order or waitlist page taking real intent.
- **services**: a productised package with one sample deliverable plus a bookable intake page.

## A note on the legal bits

Skills that produce legal or data-protection documents generate drafts to get you moving. They
are drafts. Have a qualified lawyer review anything before you rely on it. Spark does not warrant them.
