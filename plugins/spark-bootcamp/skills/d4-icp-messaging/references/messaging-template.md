## Objection bank (the usual five, use where you have no real ones yet)

- **Price.** "It costs too much / I have no budget." Answer against the hours or losses the
  ICP carries today. A price framed against a bigger cost stops being the objection.
- **Trust.** "Why you, why now, who else uses this?" Answer with a named result, a
  testimonial, or your own track record. New firms answer with the founder's own credibility.
- **Switching effort.** "I would have to change everything." Answer with how little the buyer
  changes to start. Shrink the first step until it is trivially small.
- **Does it actually work.** "Prove it." Answer with the demo, the deployed slice, the render,
  or the sample deliverable and the number it hits.
- **Timing.** "Not right now." Answer with the cost of waiting, or offer a small first step
  that fits this quarter.

## Worked example (services, Jersey trust firm)

**ICP.** A compliance lead at a Jersey trust firm of 20 to 80 staff who files periodic client
reviews by hand.

**One-liner.** We help Jersey trust compliance leads clear periodic reviews in a day, not a
fortnight, without new software. (17 words.)

**Objection 1, price.** "Two grand is a lot for a small firm." A single late review risks a
JFSC finding worth far more than that, and today the lead spends 9 days a quarter on it. The
package pays for itself in the first cycle. Evidence: strong.

Note how every answer carries a number or a named risk, and the "does it actually work"
answer points at the sample deliverable. That is the bar. Match it or flag the gap red.
