# Interview Target Spec

_Pre-work triage, 2 to 4 weeks before the bootcamp. Fill every field. Delete the guidance in brackets as you go._

## Segment (one only)
People who [do this job] at [this kind of place].

[Example: operations managers at Jersey fund administrators with 20 to 80 staff. Name a job and a place. One segment, not three.]

## Role
[The exact job title of the person who feels the pain. "Head of compliance", not "someone in compliance".]

## Buyer
[Who signs the invoice. Write "same as user" if it is the same person, or name the separate role and where they sit.]

## Target number and reason
Target: [N] interviews.
Reason: [one sentence. Why this many. If user and buyer differ, split it, e.g. "8 users plus 3 buyers, so I hear the pain and the purchase decision".]

## Screening questions (three)
Each confirms someone is genuinely in the segment. At least one must be able to return a "no".

1. [Question] → qualifies if they answer [expected answer].
2. [Question] → qualifies if they answer [expected answer].
3. [Question] → disqualifies if they answer [answer that rules them out].

## Riskiest assumption check
Assumption being tested (copied from the idea brief): [assumption].
Can these exact people prove or kill it? [Yes / No, and what you changed if no.]

## Parked for later (optional)
[Any second segment you deliberately set aside this week, so you do not lose it.]
