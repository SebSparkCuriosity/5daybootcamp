# Product-test prompt sheet (keep open during the session)

This is your live companion for a product test on Day 4. You are watching a real prospect try the thing you built: the live page, the prototype, or the sample deliverable and its intake page. The job is to see whether it moves them forward and whether they will commit. Say little. Watch what they do.

## First 60 seconds
Read the consent block, log the four fields, then set the frame: "I am testing the page, not you. Anything confusing is my fault to fix. Think out loud as you go." Do not demo it for them. Hand it over and go quiet.

## The one task
Give them a single, real task and then stop talking:
- software: "Show me how you would [do the core action] on this page."
- hardware: "Here is the prototype and the pre-order page. Show me what you would do if this were live."
- services: "Here is a sample of the work and the booking page. Walk me through whether you would book it."

## While they work (bite your tongue)
- Let silence sit. Do not rescue them when they pause; the pause is the finding.
- "What are you thinking right now?"
- "What did you expect to happen there?"
- Note the exact step where they hesitate, misread, or stall. That step is your fix list.

## When they reach the price or the commitment
This is the real test. Watch the reaction, do not soften it.
- "What went through your mind when you saw the price?"
- Then make the ask for real: sign the waitlist, place the pre-order, book the intake, commit the deposit.
- Say nothing while they decide. Their action is the score.

## Score the buying signal, 0 to 5
Score what they DID, not what they said. Full rubric in `buying-signal-rubric.md`. In short: 0 polite, 1 interest, 2 time, 3 reputation (an intro or reference), 4 intent (signed with real details), 5 money now. Write one line of evidence: the specific action that earns the number.

## Traps to avoid
- Do not lead them through it. If you have to point, that is a finding about the page, not help.
- Do not argue with confusion. Note it and move on.
- Do not count "I love it" as a signal. Love is free. Score the action.

## While you watch, capture
Their exact words in quote marks, three or more for the record. Capture the moment of confusion and the moment they saw the price, verbatim.
