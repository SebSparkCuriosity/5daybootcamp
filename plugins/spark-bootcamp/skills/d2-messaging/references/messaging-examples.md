# Messaging examples, one per path

Use these as a shape, not a script. Steal the structure, not the words. Every
proof below is illustrative; replace it with your own real number or quote.

---

## Software example (fund admin reconciliation tool)

**One-liner (9 words)**
We reconcile fund positions in minutes, not half a day.

**Key messages**
1. Care first. Claim: your team stops losing mornings to spreadsheets. -> Proof: cut reconciliation from 3 hours to 8 minutes in the working slice.
2. Trust. Claim: every match is logged and reversible. -> Proof: full changelog, human sign-off before anything posts.
3. Mechanics. Claim: it plugs into what you already use. -> Proof: reads your existing CSV export, no migration.

**Ranked order**
1, then 2, then 3. Finance buyers care about time saved, then about audit, then about setup.

**Pitch (61 words)**
Fund admins lose the first three hours of every day to reconciliation. We built a tool that does it in eight minutes and logs every match, so your team spends the morning on clients, not spreadsheets. It reads the export you already produce, so there is nothing to migrate. Can I show you a five-minute demo on your own data this week?

## Hardware example (cold-chain sensor for food logistics)

**One-liner (10 words)**
We tell you the moment a cold shipment goes wrong.

**Key messages**
1. Care first. Claim: you stop writing off spoiled loads you found out about too late. -> Proof: one Jersey distributor lost 4,000 pounds last quarter to a breach nobody saw.
2. Trust. Claim: proof for your customers and your auditor. -> Proof: tamper-evident temperature log per pallet.
3. Mechanics. Claim: no wiring, no fuss. -> Proof: battery sensor, sticks inside the crate, 90-day charge.

**Ranked order**
1, then 3, then 2. For hardware the "how hard is it to fit?" objection comes early, so mechanics jumps ahead of audit.

**Pitch (59 words)**
Chilled shipments fail quietly, and you find out when the customer rejects the pallet. Our sensor sits in the crate and alerts you the moment temperature breaks, with a tamper-evident log per pallet. No wiring, 90-day battery. One distributor lost four thousand pounds last quarter to a breach nobody caught. Want to join the waitlist and trial three units?

## Services example (productised risk pack for trust firms)

**One-liner (8 words)**
Board-ready risk packs, fixed fee, five days.

**Key messages**
1. Care first. Claim: your board pack stops eating a fortnight of senior time. -> Proof: we turn it round in 5 working days for a fixed fee.
2. Trust. Claim: it lands the way your regulator expects. -> Proof: mapped to the JFSC codes, reviewed by a qualified compliance lead.
3. Mechanics. Claim: you hand over what you have, we do the rest. -> Proof: one 30-minute intake call, one sample pack to see first.

**Ranked order**
1, then 2, then 3. Time and cost first, regulatory fit second, because that is the order trust firms actually decide in.

**Pitch (62 words)**
Preparing a board risk pack swallows two weeks of your senior people every quarter. We turn one round in five working days for a fixed fee, mapped to the JFSC codes and reviewed by a qualified compliance lead. You give us what you have on one 30-minute call. Shall I send you a sample pack so you can see the standard first?
