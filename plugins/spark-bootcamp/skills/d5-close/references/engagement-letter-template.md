# Engagement letter template

This is a draft. Have a qualified lawyer review it before you rely on it. Spark does not warrant it.

Keep it to one page. A founder's first engagement letter should be short, plain and signable in
one read. Replace every {{placeholder}}. Delete the pilot clause if the tier is not a pilot.

may stop with nothing further owed.

**What we need from you.** {{client_inputs}}, by {{input_deadline}}, so we can hit the dates above.

**How we handle your data.** We process only what we need to do this work, we keep it confidential,

for your records.

**Changes and stopping.** If the scope changes we will agree the new price in writing before we do
the extra work. Either of us may end this engagement with {{notice_period}} written notice; you pay
for work done up to that point.

**This is not legal advice and this letter is a starting point.** Please have your own adviser look
at it if anything matters to you.
