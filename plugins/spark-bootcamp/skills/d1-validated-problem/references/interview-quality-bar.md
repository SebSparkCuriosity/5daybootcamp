# What counts as a solid interview

You need 5 solid interviews before you can make the persevere-or-pivot call. This is the bar. Count only the ones that clear all four tests.

## The four tests

1. Real person in your segment. They do the job, at the kind of place, that your interview-target-spec named. Not a friend doing you a favour. Not someone adjacent who "knows the sort of thing".

2. First-hand, specific answers. They told you what they actually did last time, with detail: the tool, the workaround, the hour it cost. "I suppose that would be useful" is not first-hand. "Last Thursday I spent two hours reconciling that by hand" is.

3. Ten minutes or more of real talk about the problem. A thirty-second yes-or-no is not an interview.

4. They could prove or kill your assumption. If nothing they said could ever have changed your mind, it was not a test, it was a sales pitch you gave yourself.

## Common ways founders miscount

Counting the same enthusiastic contact twice because they introduced you to a colleague. Count people, not conversations.

Counting people who love the idea but sit outside the segment. Warmth from the wrong person is noise.

Counting an email reply with one line. Written answers count only if they gave specific, first-hand detail.
