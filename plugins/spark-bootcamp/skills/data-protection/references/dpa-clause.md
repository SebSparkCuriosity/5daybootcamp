## Data protection

1. **Roles.** Where {{BUSINESS_NAME}} processes personal data on the client's behalf to deliver this engagement, the client is the data controller and {{BUSINESS_NAME}} is the processor. Where {{BUSINESS_NAME}} decides why and how data is processed (for example our own billing records), {{BUSINESS_NAME}} is the controller for that data.

2. **Scope.** {{BUSINESS_NAME}} will process personal data only to perform this engagement, only on the client's documented instructions, and for no other purpose. The categories of data and data subjects, and the duration of processing, are those set out in the statement of work.

3. **Security.** {{BUSINESS_NAME}} will apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect the data. By default the data is held in local files under {{BUSINESS_NAME}}'s control. It is exported to a cloud service only where necessary to deliver the engagement, and only to providers engaged under a written agreement offering equivalent protection.

4. **Confidentiality.** Anyone {{BUSINESS_NAME}} allows to process the data is bound by a duty of confidence.

5. **Sub-processors.** {{BUSINESS_NAME}} will not engage a sub-processor without the client's prior written agreement (which may be a general authorisation listing approved providers), and will impose the same obligations on any sub-processor it uses.

6. **Transfers.** {{BUSINESS_NAME}} will not transfer personal data outside Jersey or the UK without a lawful basis and recognised safeguards for that transfer.

7. **Assistance.** {{BUSINESS_NAME}} will help the client respond to data-subject requests, and to security incidents, and will notify the client without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal-data breach involving the client's data.

8. **Return or deletion.** On the client's request, and at the end of the engagement, {{BUSINESS_NAME}} will return or securely delete the client's personal data, except where the law requires it to be kept.

9. **Audit.** {{BUSINESS_NAME}} will make available the information the client reasonably needs to show these obligations are met.

10. **Governing law.** This clause is governed by the Data Protection (Jersey) Law 2018 and, where it applies to the client's data, the UK GDPR.
