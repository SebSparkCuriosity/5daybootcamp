# Consent Wording (DRAFT)

{{DISCLAIMER}}

Prepared by {{BUSINESS_NAME}} ({{FOUNDER}}) on {{DATE}}. Consent has to be a clear, active choice: no pre-ticked boxes, no burying it in the small print, and easy to withdraw. Read each block to the person, or show it on the page, and record their answer.

## Interview consent (read aloud or show before you start)
"Thanks for making time. Before we begin: I would like to take notes, and I may record this call so I do not miss anything. The notes stay in local files under my control. I will use them only to understand the problem I am researching. I will not share your name or anything that identifies you outside this work without asking you first. You can stop at any time, skip any question, and ask me to delete your notes afterwards. Is that alright, and are you happy for me to record?"

Record: name, date, consent given (yes or no), recording allowed (yes or no). If they say no to recording, take written notes only. Keep this record in local files, not in the cloud, unless the person has agreed to a specific tool.

## Usability-test consent (read aloud or show before a test)
"I am testing the page, not you. There are no wrong answers, and anything confusing is my fault to fix. I would like to watch what you do and hear your thoughts out loud. I may record the screen and your voice. The recording stays in local files under my control and is used only to improve the page. You can stop at any time and ask me to delete the recording. Are you happy to carry on, and is recording OK?"

Record the same four fields as above.

## Marketing and follow-up opt-in (for the landing page or a form)
Use a single unticked checkbox with plain wording:

- [ ] I am happy for {{BUSINESS_NAME}} to email me about this. I can unsubscribe at any time.

Do not tick it for them. Do not make it a condition of getting the thing they came for. Keep a dated record of who opted in and when.

## Withdrawing consent
Tell people how, and mean it: "To withdraw consent or have your data deleted, email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. We will action it within one month and confirm when it is done." Withdrawing must be as easy as giving it, and it never costs the person anything.
