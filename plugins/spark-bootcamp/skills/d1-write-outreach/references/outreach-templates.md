# Outreach templates

Adapt every one of these to your own voice. Keep them short. The Day 4 tester line goes in all of them.

Placeholders: `{first_name}` their name, `{you}` your name, `{hook}` the specific reason you picked them, `{topic}` the pain you are exploring, `{slot_or_link}` your booking method.

---

## Cold

Subject: Learning from you, {first_name}? 20 minutes

Hi {first_name},

I picked you because {hook}. I am {you}, and I am spending this week learning how people who do your job handle {topic}. I am not selling anything. I would genuinely value 20 minutes to hear how it actually works for you.

Would {slot_or_link} suit?

One more thing: I am building a rough version of a tool around this on Thursday. Would you be up for a two-minute look and telling me if it is any good?

Thanks either way,
{you}

---

## Warm

Subject: Quick favour, {first_name}

Hi {first_name},

Good to reconnect. I am digging into {topic} this week and you are exactly the right person to learn from. Could I borrow 20 minutes?

{slot_or_link}

Also: I will have a rough build to show on Thursday. Fancy giving it two minutes and a verdict?

Cheers,
{you}

---

## Intro-request

Send this to a mutual contact. Write it so they can forward it as-is.

Subject: Could you put me in touch with {first_name}?

Hi {mutual},

I am learning about {topic} this week and {first_name} at {their_firm} would be a brilliant person to hear from. Would you be happy to introduce us? Feel free to forward the paragraph below.

---
"{first_name}, {you} is spending this week learning how people handle {topic}. Not selling anything, just 20 minutes to learn from you. Would you be open to it?"
---

Thank you,
{you}

---

## Follow-up

Send once, three to four working days after the first message. No guilt.

Subject: Re: Learning from you, {first_name}?

Hi {first_name},

Just floating this back up in case it slipped past. Still keen to learn from you, and happy to work around your diary, even 15 minutes helps.

{slot_or_link}

Thanks,
{you}
