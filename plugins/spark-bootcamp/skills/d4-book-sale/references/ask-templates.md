# Ask templates: booking the Friday meeting

Every template obeys the same five-beat structure. Keep the whole message under 120 words.

1. **Context**: one line on who you are and why you are writing to them specifically.
2. **Reason it helps them**: the single sentence of pain you remove. Their benefit, not your product.
3. **The ask**: 20 minutes, two specific Friday times, a format.
4. **Low friction**: make saying yes a one-word reply.
5. **Exit**: one line that lets them decline without awkwardness. This raises reply rates, it does not lower them.

Rules: no attachments, no deck, no links unless they ask. Ask to learn, not to sell. British English. No em dashes.

---

## Email (finance, trust, law, accountancy, fund admin)

Subject: 20 minutes on {pain area}?

Hi {first name},

{One line of context: how you know them or why them.} I am {your name}, working on {one-line proposition}.

I am talking to a handful of {their role}s this week about {the pain}, and your view would genuinely help me get it right.

Could I borrow 20 minutes on Friday {date}, either {time A} or {time B}? Video or your office, whichever suits.

If the timing is wrong, no problem at all, just say and I will not chase.

Thanks,
{Your name}

---

## LinkedIn / short message (professional services)

Hi {first name}, I am {your name}, working on {one-line proposition}. I am speaking to a few {their role}s this week about {the pain} and would value 20 minutes of your thinking. Friday {date}, {time A} or {time B}? Happy to work around you. If not, no worries at all.

---

## Text / WhatsApp (hospitality, construction, trades)

Hi {first name}, {your name} here. Building something to fix {the pain} for {their kind of business}. Could I grab 20 mins Friday, {time A} or {time B}, to get your take? No sell, just questions. All good if not.

---

## The confirmation line (send the moment they say yes)

Brilliant, thank you. Friday {date} at {time}, {format}. I will send a calendar invite now so it is in your diary. I will keep it to 20 minutes.

---

## The one nudge (Thursday afternoon, if silent, send once only)

Hi {first name}, just floating this back up in case it slipped past. Still very keen on 20 minutes Friday if you can spare it, {time A} or {time B}. If now is not the moment, genuinely no problem.
