# Payment Setup

**The recommendation: bank transfer, 50% deposit on signature, balance on delivery.** This is how you get money in the account the same day someone says yes.

## Why bank transfer, not card

A local sterling bank transfer clears the same day. Card and direct-debit onboarding does not. Opening a Stripe or GoCardless account and getting it verified for a brand new business can take more than a day, sometimes several, because they run identity and business checks before they let you take money. That is fine for month two. It is no good on a Friday when a customer has just said yes and you want the deposit before the weekend.

So for your first sale: **transfer first, cards later.**

| Method | Time to first cleared payment | Verdict for this week |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bank transfer | Same day (local sterling) | Use this. |
| Card (Stripe) | 1 day or more to verify a new account | Add later. |
| Direct debit (GoCardless) | 1 day or more to verify, then days per collection | Add later, good for recurring. |
| Cash / cheque | Slow, awkward, hard to audit | Avoid. |

## The deposit

Ask for **{{DEPOSIT_PCT}} on signature, balance on delivery.** On a {{PRICE}} engagement that is {{DEPOSIT_AMOUNT}} now and {{BALANCE_AMOUNT}} on delivery.

The deposit does two jobs. It gets real cash in this week, which is the whole point of Day 5. And it filters tyre-kickers: a buyer who will not pay a deposit was never going to pay the balance. You start work when the deposit clears, not before.

## Set it up, in order

1. Confirm the account name on the invoice matches your trading entity exactly. A payment to a name that does not match the invoice gets held or bounced.
2. Put the full details on the invoice: account name, bank, sort code or local clearing, account number, IBAN, BIC and a payment reference (use the invoice number).
3. Send the pack (letter, terms, invoice) only after sign-off. Ask for the deposit against the invoice reference.
4. Watch for the deposit to clear. Start work when it lands, not on the promise.
5. Once this sale is in, set up Stripe or GoCardless in the background for the next customer, so month two you can take cards and recurring payments without the wait.

## If you cannot get a transfer set up in time

Fallback: get the engagement letter signed and an agreed payment date in writing within seven days. The yes is locked either way. Do not let a missing bank detail lose you a customer who is ready to pay.
