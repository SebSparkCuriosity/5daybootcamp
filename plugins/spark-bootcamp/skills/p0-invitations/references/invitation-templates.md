# Invitation templates

Adapt every one of these to the founder's own voice. Keep them short. The interview date goes in the first three lines of every message; a slot in the diary is the whole point.

Placeholders: `{first_name}` their name, `{you}` your name, `{hook}` the specific reason you picked them, `{topic}` the pain you are exploring, `{date}` the interview day (e.g. "Monday 14 September"), `{overflow}` the backup window (e.g. "Tuesday morning the 15th"), `{slot_or_link}` your booking method.

---

## Email: cold

Subject: Learning from you, {first_name}? 20 minutes on {date}

Hi {first_name},

I picked you because {hook}. I am {you}, and on {date} I am spending the day learning how people who do your job handle {topic}. I am not selling anything. I would genuinely value 20 minutes of your day to hear how it actually works for you.

Would a slot on {date} suit, or {overflow} if that is easier? {slot_or_link}

PS: by the Thursday of that week I will have built something rough around this. If you would give it a two-minute look and an honest verdict, I would value that too.

Thanks either way,
{you}

---

## Email: warm

Subject: Quick favour, {first_name}: 20 minutes on {date}

Hi {first_name},

Good to reconnect. On {date} I am digging into {topic} and you are exactly the right person to learn from. Could I borrow 20 minutes that day, or {overflow}?

{slot_or_link}

PS: I will have a rough build to show by that Thursday. Fancy giving it two minutes and a verdict?

Cheers,
{you}

---

## Email: intro-request

Send this to a mutual contact. Write it so they can forward it as-is.

Subject: Could you put me in touch with {first_name}?

Hi {mutual},

On {date} I am learning about {topic} and {first_name} at {their_firm} would be a brilliant person to hear from. Would you be happy to introduce us? Feel free to forward the paragraph below.

---
"{first_name}, {you} is spending {date} learning how people handle {topic}. Not selling anything, just 20 minutes to learn from you. Would you be open to it?"
---

Thank you,
{you}

---

## WhatsApp

For hot and warm contacts only. Two sentences and the link. No PS.

> Hi {first_name}, it's {you}. On {date} I'm spending the day learning how people handle {topic} and you're one of the first people I thought of. Could I borrow 20 minutes that day (or {overflow})? {slot_or_link}

---

## LinkedIn

Connection note (the 300-character limit is real, count it):

> Hi {first_name}, {hook}. On {date} I'm learning how people in your role handle {topic}, not selling anything. Could I borrow 20 minutes that day? I'll send times once we're connected. {you}

Once connected, or for existing connections, send the warm email text as a message with the booking link.

---

## Follow-up one

Send once, 4 to 5 working days after the first message, on the same channel. No guilt.

> Hi {first_name}, just floating this back up in case it slipped past. Still keen to learn from you on {date}, and happy to work around your diary, even 15 minutes helps. {slot_or_link}

## Follow-up two (final)

Send one week after follow-up one, then stop and replace the name from the invite list.

> Hi {first_name}, last nudge, I promise. If {date} doesn't work I'll leave you in peace, but if you can spare 15 minutes I'd still love to learn from you. {slot_or_link}
