**Client:** [Prospect organisation]
**Attendees:** [Your name], [Client contact(s)]

**SLA:** held within 5 working days of the signed engagement letter.
**Length:** 30 minutes. Keep it tight. This is a paid meeting, not a chat.

## Before the call
- [ ] Engagement letter signed and filed.
- [ ] Deposit received (if your terms take one).
- [ ] You have read the proposal back so the scope is fresh.

## Agenda (30 minutes)
1. **Confirm the outcome (5 min).** Restate the numeric promise from the proposal. Get the client to agree, out loud, that this is what "done" means.
2. **Confirm the one must-work thing (10 min).**
   - Software: the single core action the slice must let their user complete.
   - Hardware: the exact spec the prototype is demonstrating and pre-ordering against.
   - Services: the brief for the sample deliverable, pinned down so it lands first time.
3. **Confirm what you need from them (5 min).** Access, information, a sign-off contact. Agree the date each arrives.
4. **Confirm the delivery date (5 min).** State it. Get agreement.
5. **Confirm the next contact (5 min).** When you next speak, and how (call, email, live demo).

## After the call
- [ ] Send a 5-line recap within 1 working day: the outcome, the one must-work thing, what you need and by when, the delivery date.
- [ ] Log the kickoff in `CHANGELOG.md`.
