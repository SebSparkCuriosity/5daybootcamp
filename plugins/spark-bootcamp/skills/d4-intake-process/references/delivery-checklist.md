**Client:** [Prospect organisation]
**Owner of this stage:** [Your name]
**SLA:** first delivery within the window promised in the proposal ([N] working days from kickoff).

Tick every box in front of the client. Delivery is not "I think it is done". Delivery is the client agreeing it is done.

## Acceptance line
The single test that decides whether this is delivered:
- **Software:** the prospect can reach the public URL and complete the core action once, unaided.
- **Hardware:** the prospect has seen the working mock or CAD render and their payment intent (pre-order or waitlist) is captured.
- **Services:** the prospect has the sample deliverable and has confirmed it matches the brief agreed at kickoff.

Keep only the line that fits your `business_type`. Delete the other two.

## Checklist
- [ ] The deliverable matches the scope in the proposal, no more and no less.
- [ ] The acceptance line above is met and the client has confirmed it, in writing.
- [ ] The numeric promise from the proposal is met, or the gap is named and agreed.
- [ ] Any handover material (login, file, spec sheet, instructions) is with the client.
- [ ] The invoice is sent, or the final payment is triggered per the engagement letter.

## After delivery
- [ ] Ask the one question that turns a first sale into a second: "What would make you recommend this to someone else?"
- [ ] Log the delivery in `CHANGELOG.md` with the result.
- [ ] Record any lesson worth keeping in `DECISIONS.md`.
