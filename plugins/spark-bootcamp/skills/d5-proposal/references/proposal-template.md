# Proposal template

Fill every bracket. Delete every bracket. Keep the whole thing on one page:
aim for 250 to 450 words. If it runs long, cut the problem section first, not
the target or the price. One number, one timeline, one price, one next step.

Keep the six parts in this order. The buyer should be able to say yes without
scrolling.

---

**To:** [Buyer name], [role], [firm]
**From:** [Your name], [your firm]
**Date:** [DD Month YYYY]

## The problem

[Two or three sentences, in the buyer's own words, from validated-problem.md.
Name the workflow that hurts and what it currently costs them, in time or money
or risk. No solution yet. Make them nod.]

## What you get

[One or two sentences naming the outcome, from proposition.md. Lead with the
number. This is the target, stated as a before and after.]

**Target: [from X] to [Y], [by when].** [For example: month-end close from 3
days to half a day, inside four weeks.]

[Then one line on the shape of the delivery, matched to your path:]
- software: a working slice live on [URL], doing [the one job].
- hardware: a [prototype or pre-order page] proving [the one thing], taking real intent.
- services: a [named sample deliverable] plus a bookable intake, so you see the standard before you commit.

## How and when

[One short paragraph. The delivery window, always inside six weeks. Name the
milestone the buyer will see, and when. Human in the loop, changelogged, so your
compliance team can follow every step. For example: "Week 1 you sign off the
scope. Week 3 you see the working slice on your own data. Week 4 it is live and
handed over, with a full changelog."]

## The price

**[One price. GBP, fixed. For example: GBP 4,500, fixed.]** [One line on what it
covers and what it does not. No options, no ranges, no day rates. If you must
note a follow-on, do it in a single sentence, not a second price.]

## Next step

[One action, one owner, one date. For example: "Say yes by Friday and we start
Monday 20 July." Or, if this rides on the Friday meeting: "We talk Friday at
10:00 for 20 minutes, then you decide."]
