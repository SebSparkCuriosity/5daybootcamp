# How To Run A Discovery Call

A short guide to running the call in `interview-script.md`. Read it once before your first interview. Four moments matter: how you open, how you avoid pitching, how you record and transcribe, and how you close.

## How to open
Thank them and name the time you will take (25 to 30 minutes is right). Ask consent to record before anything else. Then say the thing that unlocks honesty: "I am not selling anything, I am trying to learn how you work." People relax the moment they know they are not a target.

## How to avoid pitching
This is the hard part, so plan for it. Keep your idea in your pocket for the whole call. When they ask "so what are you building?", say you are still figuring it out and turn it back: "still working that out, but tell me how you handle it today." If you catch yourself explaining your idea, stop mid-sentence and ask "how do you handle that now?". You are not here to be liked. You are here to find out whether the problem is real and painful.

Whatever you choose, the workflow is the same: get a transcript, then paste it into run-interview, which turns it into a clean structured record with the quotes pulled out. Do not try to write the record live. Listen on the call, transcribe after.

## How to close
Two questions, every time. First: "who else doing your kind of job should I speak to?" This is how your contact list grows without cold outreach. Second: "can I come back to you once I have spoken to a few more people?" Then thank them and stop the recording.

## After the call
Within the hour, before the memory fades, run run-interview and paste your transcript. The longer you leave it, the more you lose.
