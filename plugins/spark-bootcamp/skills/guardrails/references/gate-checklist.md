# Gate checklist: exact wording to put in front of the founder

Use these when a skill reaches a gated action. Fill the brackets with the real values. Do not paraphrase the ask for approval: the founder must see the real thing and give a clear yes.

## Send gate
Show the exact final message and the exact recipient list, then ask:

> This sends [N] [emails / messages] from [your address / your account] to the [N] people listed above. I have shown you the exact text and every name. Nothing goes out until you reply with your approval. Send them?

Wait for an unambiguous yes. If the founder edits anything, show the new version in full and ask again. Approval covers this batch only.

## Spend gate
Name the amount, the currency, whether it recurs, and the free alternative if one exists, then ask:

> This costs [GBP X] [once / per month]. There is [a free route: name it / no free route that meets the same target]. Nothing is charged until you approve this specific amount. Go ahead?

Wait for approval of that exact figure. A new amount needs a new yes.

## Publish gate
Show the final content and the live URL, confirm the privacy note and any draft disclaimer are present, then ask:

> This goes live at [URL] where any prospect can find it and act on it. [It collects emails, so the privacy note is in place. / It carries the draft-review disclaimer.] It is not public until you approve. Publish it?

A private preview URL for the founder's own eyes does not need this. Flipping it public does.

## Number gate
Before any market or financial figure lands in an artefact, attach one of:

- **Source:** `[figure] (source: [specific, checkable source])`. Example: `roughly 13,000 finance-sector jobs in Jersey (source: Jersey Finance 2024 annual review)`.
- **Assumption flag:** `[figure] (assumption: [reasoning], not yet verified)`. Example: `around 40 trust firms in Jersey (assumption: based on the JFSC register count, not yet verified)`.

If you have neither, do not write the number. Find a source, flag it as an assumption, or drop it.

## Data-export opt-in
When a skill offers to move interview or customer data off the local machine, ask:

> This copies [what] to [where, and whose account]. By default this data stays in local files in your project. Do you want to export it for this step? It stays local if you say no.

Opt-in is per step. It is never remembered as a standing yes.

## Regulated-sector disclaimer (verbatim)
Attach to every legal, compliance, or data-protection output, unchanged:

> This is a draft. Have a qualified lawyer review it before you rely on it. Spark does not warrant it.

## The one rule when unsure
If you cannot tell whether an action is gated, treat it as gated and ask the founder. Pausing costs seconds. Not pausing can cost a prospect, a charge, or a figure no one can defend.
