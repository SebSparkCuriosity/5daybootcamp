# Conversion benchmarks: the assumption ladder

Read this once. These are starting assumptions, not promises. Label every number
in your funnel as either an assumption (from this page) or a real number (from
your own results). Replace an assumption the moment you have a real figure. Never
present an assumption as a fact, to a buyer, an investor or yourself.

## Why you need this

To land one paying customer you must reach enough strangers at the top that the
survivors down the funnel still leave one buyer at the bottom. Multiply the
pass-through rates and you get the number you must reach on day one. If that
number is bigger than your reachable pool, the funnel is wrong, not the market.

## Default pass-through assumptions (cold channels)

These are conservative planning defaults for a cold, low-trust channel. Sources:
widely reported B2B funnel ranges; treat as an order-of-magnitude guide, not
gospel.

| From stage | To stage | Assumed pass-through |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Awareness | Interest | 5% |
| Interest | Consideration | 30% |
| Consideration | Purchase | 20% |

Worked example. To get 1 purchase:
- Purchase 1, so Consideration needs 1 / 0.20 = 5.
- Consideration 5, so Interest needs 5 / 0.30 = 17.
- Interest 17, so Awareness needs 17 / 0.05 = 340.

So a cold funnel needs roughly **340 strangers reached** to land one sale. Write
that number in funnel.md and log it as your result.

## Warm channels change the maths completely

A warm introduction or a referral does not start cold. Rough warm defaults:

| From stage | To stage | Assumed pass-through (warm) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Awareness | Interest | 40% |
| Interest | Consideration | 60% |
| Consideration | Purchase | 40% |

Worked example. To get 1 purchase: Consideration 1 / 0.40 = 3, Interest 3 / 0.60
= 5, Awareness 5 / 0.40 = 13. So a warm funnel needs roughly **13 warm contacts**
to land one sale, against 340 cold. That is why the Jersey channels reference
tells you to favour warm. In a small market you often cannot find 340 reachable
strangers, but you can find 13 warm introductions in a week.

## How to use these numbers

1. Pick the ladder that matches your chosen channel: cold or warm.
2. Work back from 1 sale to the top-of-funnel number.
3. Check that number against your reachable pool. If it does not fit, switch to a
   warmer channel or widen the segment. Do not tweak the percentages to make the
   sum look nicer. That is fabrication.
4. As real numbers arrive during the week, overwrite the assumptions and recompute.
