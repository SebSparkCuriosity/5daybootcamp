# Jersey channels: where to run each funnel stage

Jersey is small. The whole island is roughly 100,000 people and a few thousand
firms. That is the point. You cannot win on volume, so win on trust and
proximity. Warm beats cold every time here, because everyone is two
introductions from everyone else.

Pick one channel per stage. This is a shortlist to choose from, not a list to
run all at once.

## Awareness (a stranger learns you exist)

- **A warm introduction from a mutual contact.** The single highest-trust
  channel on the island. One good introducer is worth a month of cold posts.
- **LinkedIn, posting into the Jersey finance and professional community.** Most
  finance, trust, law and fund people are active here. Post to be seen by name.
- **Jersey First for Finance, Digital Jersey and the sector bodies.** Events,
  member lists and newsletters reach exactly the regulated firms you want.
- **Professional bodies: Jersey Chamber of Commerce, ICAEW, STEP Jersey, the
  Jersey Law Society.** Their events and directories are trusted front doors.
- **Local press for reach, not conversion: Bailiwick Express, Jersey Evening
  Post.** Good for credibility, weak for direct action. Treat it as top-of-funnel
  air cover.

Avoid broad paid social at the awareness stage. You will pay to reach the UK and
the world when your buyer is a few hundred firms down the road.

## Interest (they give you a way to reach them)

- **A one-to-one coffee or a booked call.** Jersey runs on face-to-face. Offer it.
- **A short explainer page with a single email capture.** One promise, one field.
- **A talk or a stand at a sector event.** You leave with names, not applause.

## Consideration (they weigh you up)

- **A live demo, a sample deliverable or a spec sheet**, matched to your path
  (see the branch in the skill).
- **A named referral or reference from a peer firm.** In a small market, "who
  else uses you?" is the deciding question. Line up one referee early.

## Purchase (they commit money)

- **A direct paid pilot, pre-order or booked intake.** Keep the payment step on
  the island's terms: clear pricing, a human to talk to, an audit trail.

## The regulated-market rule

Finance, trust, fund and legal buyers move on trust and on being able to show
their own regulator a clean process. Favour channels where trust already exists
(referral, professional body, named referee) over channels that only deliver
volume. Fewer, warmer, better.
